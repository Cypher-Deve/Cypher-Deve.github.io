Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Vexil ("the App", "we", "our") is a flag-guessing game developed and operated by an independent developer, Cypher (contact: Cypher.deve@gmail.com). This policy explains what data the App collects, what we do with it, and your choices.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

To remember your progress, the App saves a small amount of data locally on your device: your settings (such as sound preferences), your win streak and game statistics, your progress in the current Daily Challenge, and notification timing information. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

2. Online Leaderboard

The App has an online leaderboard. To run it, we store the following on our backend, hosted by Supabase (https://supabase.com/privacy):

  • A nickname you choose. Your nickname and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboard, so please do not use your real name or any personal information.
  • A device identifier (on Android, the Android ID), used only to link your scores and stats to your device so they survive a reinstall and to prevent duplicate entries.
  • Your game scores and statistics (such as streaks and results).

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location, and the App has no accounts or sign-in.

Deleting your data. To have your nickname, device identifier, and scores removed from our leaderboard, email us at Cypher.deve@gmail.com from any address and tell us your nickname. We will delete the data within a reasonable time.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device and usage data (such as advertising ID, IP address, device type, and general usage statistics) to serve and measure ads. This data collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

To fill ad requests, AdMob may work with another ad network through mediation. This currently includes Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Unity may also collect device and usage data (such as advertising ID, IP address, and device information) to serve and measure ads, under its own privacy policy:

https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where consent is required, the App shows a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in the App's settings. Everywhere, you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

4. Notifications

The App may send a local, on-device notification to let you know when a new Daily Challenge is available. A periodic background check on your device decides whether to show it. The check and the notification run entirely on your device and are not tied to any external service or account. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

5. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require account creation, sign-in, or personal information such as your name, email, or phone number
  • We do not access your photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or location
  • We do not sell your data. The only data shared with third parties is the ad-serving data collected by Google AdMob and its mediation partner (Unity Ads), and the leaderboard data stored with our backend provider (Supabase), as described above

6. Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions:

  • Internet access — for the leaderboard and ads
  • Notifications — used for optional Daily Challenge reminders; can be disabled in your device settings at any time
  • Run at startup — lets the reminder schedule resume after your device restarts

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). Ads shown in the App are served by Google AdMob and its mediation partner under their own policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: Cypher.deve@gmail.com
