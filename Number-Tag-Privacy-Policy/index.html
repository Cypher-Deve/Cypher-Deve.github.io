Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Number Tag ("the App", "we", "our") is a two-player number-finding game developed and operated by an independent developer, Cypher (contact: Cypher.deve@gmail.com). This policy explains what data the App collects, what we do with it, and your choices.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

To remember your preferences, the App saves a small amount of data locally on your device: your settings (such as sound, haptic feedback, and your default match timer) and your username. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

2. Username

The App asks for a username, which is used as your name in every game mode and shown to your opponent in online matches. Usernames are unique: when you save one, it is sent to our backend (when you have a connection) so that nobody else can use the same name. Please do not use your real name or any personal information as your username.

A username that is not used for about 90 days is released automatically so that someone else can claim it.

3. Online Multiplayer

The App has an online mode where you play against a friend using a room code. To run it, we store the following on our backend, hosted by Supabase (https://supabase.com/privacy):

  • A random anonymous user ID, created automatically the first time you use online features. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or any other account, and it lets the server know which player is making each move.
  • Your username.
  • Match data for the room you are in: the room code, both players' usernames, the numbers called and found, clock times, and the result.
  • Short-lived counters used to limit spam and abuse (for example, how many rooms you tried to create or join in the last minute).

Match data is only kept while it is needed. Rooms are deleted automatically soon after a match ends or when players leave, and abandoned rooms are cleaned up automatically. Your opponent can see your username and your moves in the match, but nothing else about you.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location, and the App has no accounts or sign-in.

Deleting your data. To have your username and anonymous user ID removed from our backend, email us at Cypher.deve@gmail.com from any address and tell us your username. We will delete the data within a reasonable time.

4. Analytics and Crash Reports

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to find and fix crashes. These services may collect information such as app usage events (for example, that a match was started or finished), crash reports, device model, operating system version, and an app-instance identifier. This data is collected by Google and is governed by Google's privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not use this data to identify you personally.

5. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device and usage data (such as advertising ID, IP address, device type, and general usage statistics) to serve and measure ads. This data collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

To fill ad requests, AdMob may work with another ad network through mediation. This currently includes Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Unity may also collect device and usage data (such as advertising ID, IP address, and device information) to serve and measure ads, under its own privacy policy:

https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where consent is required, the App shows a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in the App's settings. If you live in a US state that gives you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data, the App also shows that choice and passes your decision on to Unity Ads. Everywhere, you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

6. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require account creation, sign-in, or personal information such as your name, email, or phone number
  • We do not access your photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or location
  • We do not sell your data. The only data shared with third parties is the ad-serving data collected by Google AdMob and its mediation partner (Unity Ads), the analytics and crash data collected by Google Firebase, and the username and online match data stored with our backend provider (Supabase), as described above

7. Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions:

  • Internet access — for online matches, username checks, analytics, and ads
  • Advertising ID — used by the ad services described above; you can reset it or opt out of personalization in your device's ad settings

8. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). Ads shown in the App are served by Google AdMob and its mediation partner under their own policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help resolve it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: Cypher.deve@gmail.com
